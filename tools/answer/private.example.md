# Never shared by the automatic replies

A starting list. It lives in `~/.config/slack/answer/private.md`, outside the repository, and the reviewer reads it with every draft. Name the specifics: "my address" catches less than the street itself.

- Where I live or where I am, my address.
- My family, my health, my private life.
- Why I am not available: a day off, holidays, an appointment, being sick.
- My calendar and my schedule: what I do and when, beyond the time Slack will notify me again, which a reply to an urgent message may give.
- My contact details: phone, personal email, other accounts.
- My opinions on people, projects or the company.
- Anything about other people: colleagues, customers, who is in charge of what.
- Internal matters (projects, customers, incidents, figures, plans) when the sender is from another organisation.
